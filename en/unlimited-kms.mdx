## Benefits of Unlimited KMs

Unlimited KMs removes one of the most common rental restrictions. You pay your regular rental fee and ride as needed without planning around distance caps.

### No Extra Charges

Ride **5 km or 500 km** and your **weekly rental fee stays the same**. There are **no hidden distance surcharges** based on how far you travel.

- **Predictable costs** — Your rental price does not increase because you rode more than expected.

- **No distance budgeting** — You do not need to limit trips to avoid extra fees.

- **Fewer surprises** — Your kilometres do not trigger an additional charge.

### Ideal for Delivery Riders

Unlimited KMs is especially useful if you rely on your scooter for **delivery work**. More time on the road can mean more completed jobs without added rental cost.

- **More earning potential** — Cover more ground without your rental cost increasing.

- **Better flexibility** — Accept longer routes without worrying about kilometre caps.

- **More suitable for daily use** — High-distance riding fits the rental model better when there is no usage limit.

### Freedom for All Uses

Unlimited KMs supports both **work** and **personal use**. You are not locked into a fixed distance allowance.

- **Commute freely** — Travel to and from work without counting kilometres.

- **Run errands when needed** — Use the scooter for day-to-day travel without second-guessing the cost.

- **Ride with fewer restrictions** — Your usage is not limited by a cap.

## Things to Keep in Mind

Unlimited kilometres gives you more freedom, but it does not remove your responsibility to look after the scooter properly.

### Wear and Tear Increases with Distance

More riding leads to more wear on parts that naturally degrade over time. Tyres, brakes, and oil all need closer attention when you cover more distance.

- **More kilometres means more servicing** — Heavy use usually brings service intervals around faster.

- **Parts wear normally** — Braking, cornering, and daily road use affect consumable parts over time.

- **Maintenance still matters** — Unlimited KMs works best when you keep the scooter in good condition.

### Fuel and Tyres Are Your Responsibility

You need to keep the scooter fuelled and make sure the tyres stay in proper condition during your rental. Punctures and tyre damage caused by riding are not covered under rental.

- **Top up fuel as needed** — Fuel costs remain your responsibility throughout the rental.

- **Monitor tyre condition** — Keep tyres properly maintained and check for damage.

- **Riding damage is not covered** — Punctures and tyre damage from use are your responsibility.

- **Tread-based replacement is covered** — We cover tyre replacement once tread reaches the minimum required level.

### Insurance and Traffic Rules Still Apply

Unlimited KMs does not change your legal or safety responsibilities. Ride within Queensland road rules and make sure your insurance cover matches how you use the scooter.

- **Follow road laws** — Ride safely and lawfully at all times.

- **Use the scooter appropriately** — Your riding must stay within the permitted purpose.

- **Keep insurance in mind** — Cover still depends on compliant and lawful use.

## Final Notes

Unlimited KMs means **no limits** and **no surprise distance charges**. It is one of the practical advantages of renting with Motion Rental.

Keep your scooter **well maintained**, follow **road laws**, and ride with confidence.
