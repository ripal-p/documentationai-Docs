## Refund payment method

Motion Rental processes your security bond refund through PayTo first. PayTo uses the same Direct Debit agreement you approved at the start of your rental, so in most cases Motion Rental can return your funds without needing anything else from you.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  PayTo uses the same agreement as the Direct Debit set up at the start of your rental, so there is nothing extra for you to do if that agreement is still active.
</Callout>

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If you cancel your Direct Debit agreement after returning the vehicle, Motion Rental cannot refund your security bond through PayTo and will need to process the refund manually. Manual processing may cause delays.
</Callout>

### Primary method: PayTo

Motion Rental uses PayTo, the same system that handles Direct Debit during your rental, to process your security bond refund automatically. If your PayTo or Direct Debit agreement is still active, Motion Rental sends the refund through that agreement and no action is required from you.

### Manual processing

Manual processing applies in these situations:

- Your bank does not participate in PayTo services
- You cancelled your Direct Debit agreement after returning the vehicle

If manual processing is required, Motion Rental will contact you after your rental ends to request your BSB and account number for a bank transfer.

## Refund process timeline

Refunds run in a weekly cycle, with a buffer between when you return the vehicle and when Motion Rental releases funds.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Motion Rental processes refunds **once per week**, usually on **Thursday late night or Friday morning**.
</Callout>

## 4 business day buffer rule

Refunds do not go out immediately after you return the vehicle. Motion Rental uses a fixed buffer period to finalise your account before the next weekly processing run.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Motion Rental requires a **minimum of 4 full business days** between your vehicle return date and the next Thursday / Friday refund processing window.
</Callout>

The buffer allows Motion Rental to:

- Inspect the vehicle condition after return

- Confirm fuel levels and refuelling charges (if any)

- Check for toll road usage

- Review fines and infringements linked to your rental

- Verify and settle any other outstanding amounts on your account

If Motion Rental identifies pending charges during this period, the team will complete those checks before releasing any remaining funds.

## Refund examples

These examples show how the 4 business day buffer and weekly processing cycle work together. Exact dates depend on public holidays and the specific Thursday / Friday cycle in your rental period.

- **Vehicle returned on Saturday**\
  Minimum 4 business day buffer runs from Monday to Thursday. Your refund is then included in the **upcoming Thursday night / Friday morning** cycle — roughly **6 days after return**.

- **Vehicle returned on Monday**\
  Minimum 4 business day buffer runs from Tuesday to Friday. Your refund falls into the **following week’s Thursday night / Friday morning** cycle — roughly **10–11 days after return**.

- **Vehicle returned on Wednesday**\
  Minimum 4 business day buffer runs from Thursday to the following Tuesday. Your refund is processed in the **next week’s Thursday night / Friday morning** cycle — roughly **8–9 days after return**.

If Motion Rental still needs information from you, for example bank details when PayTo is not available because your bank does not participate in PayTo or you cancelled your Direct Debit, your refund will move to the **next available Thursday / Friday cycle** after the required details are received.

## Final notes

Make refund processing as smooth and fast as possible by following these points:

- Keep your **Direct Debit agreement active** until your **security bond refund** has been processed.

- If Motion Rental requests your **BSB and account number** for a manual transfer, send those details **promptly**.

- Expect delays if Motion Rental is waiting on information from you or still finalising inspections, fuel checks, tolls, fines, infringements, or other outstanding amounts.

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="I provided my bank details late. When will I get my refund?" default-open="true">
    Motion Rental adds your refund to the **next available Thursday night / Friday morning processing cycle** after your bank details are received and the 4 business day buffer has passed. If PayTo is not available and you send bank details after the current week’s processing run, your refund will move to the following week’s cycle.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How will I know when my refund has been issued?" default-open="false">
    Motion Rental sends you a confirmation message **via WhatsApp or SMS** once your refund has been processed. After you receive this confirmation, your bank may still take some time to show the funds in your account, depending on their processing times.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if my bank doesn't support PayTo?" default-open="false">
    If your bank does not participate in PayTo services, Motion Rental will contact you after your rental ends to request your **BSB and account number** and process your refund manually.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens if I cancel my Direct Debit before getting my refund?" default-open="false">
    If you cancel your Direct Debit agreement before your refund is processed, Motion Rental cannot use PayTo to return your security bond. Motion Rental will need to process the refund manually instead, which may take longer.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why are refunds only processed once per week?" default-open="false">
    Motion Rental processes refunds once per week (Thursday night or Friday morning) to give enough time to:

    - Inspect the vehicle condition

    - Confirm fuel levels and refuelling charges

    - Check toll usage

    - Review fines and infringements

    - Verify any other pending or outstanding charges

    This weekly cycle ensures that your account is fully settled before any remaining funds are released.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
