  <Expandable title="TL;DR" default-open="true">
    As part of setting up Direct Debit, you need to make a one-time $0.99 verification payment via PayID using a custom email address we generate for you. This confirms the bank account belongs to you. After the payment, your bank account is verified and you can complete the Direct Debit setup.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## What is this payment for?

When you set up Direct Debit for your weekly rental fees, Motion Rental needs to verify that the bank account belongs to you. You do this by making a small $0.99 payment via PayID from the same bank account you want to link.

Once the payment is verified, your bank account can be linked for Direct Debit. You can then finish the setup in the app and sign the Direct Debit Request Form.

## Your PayID details

We generate a **custom PayID email address** for you as part of your setup. You should have already received this in the app or in the communication sent to you by Motion Rental.

Use these details whenever you make the $0.99 verification payment:

- **PayID type:** Email
- **PayID:** Your custom email address (check the app or your communication from us)
- **Business name:** `Motion Rentals`

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  Always confirm the business name shows exactly as `Motion Rental Pty Ltd` before you pay. If the name does not match exactly, stop and contact us immediately.
</Callout>

## Before you start

Make sure you have:

- Your custom PayID email address, provided in the app or sent to you
- The payment description provided to you in the app
- Access to the same bank account you want to use for Direct Debit
- The verification amount of $0.99

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  The $0.99 payment must come from the same bank account you want to link for Direct Debit. If you pay from a different account, the verification will not work.
</Callout>

## After making the payment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Return to the app" title-type="p">
    Go back to the app and tap **I have made the payment** to continue.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for verification" title-type="p">
    Wait a moment for the system to verify your payment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select your bank account" title-type="p">
    Once verified, your bank account will appear for selection. Select it and tap **Set Up Direct Debit**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign the Direct Debit Request Form" title-type="p">
    Sign the agreement to confirm your consent and complete the Direct Debit setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What if I can't find my PayID email address?" default-open="true">
    If you can't find your PayID email address in the app or in your communications from us, contact our support team and we'll send it to you.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why is the payment $0.99?" default-open="false">
    The $0.99 is a small verification payment that confirms the bank account belongs to you. It's a standard security step in the Direct Debit setup process.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I use a different bank account for the verification payment?" default-open="false">
    No. The $0.99 payment must come from the same bank account you want to link for Direct Debit. This is how we verify the account is yours.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What happens after I make the payment?" default-open="false">
    After you make the $0.99 payment and tap **I have made the payment** in the app, your bank account will be verified and available to select. You can then complete the Direct Debit setup by signing the Direct Debit Request Form.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="I made the payment but it's not showing as verified. What should I do?" default-open="false">
    Wait a few minutes and try tapping **I have made the payment** again. Make sure you used the correct payment description from the app and that you paid from the correct bank account. If the issue persists, contact our support team.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I use PayID (Email) for other payments like the Security Bond?" default-open="false">
    No. PayID (Email) is only used for the $0.99 verification payment during Direct Debit setup. For Security Bond, first week's subscription fee, and other manual payments, use [Pay via PayID (ABN)](/en/pay-via-payid-abn) instead.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
