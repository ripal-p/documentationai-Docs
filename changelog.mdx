## Changelog

<Update label="2026-05-06" description="New features" tags={["New"]}>
  ## Hindi language support now available

  - The full documentation site is now available in Hindi
  - All pages are translated, including getting started, rental information, payments, vehicle maintenance, and more
  - Switch to Hindi from the language selector in the navigation
</Update>

<Update label="2026-04-15" description="New features" tags={["New"]}>
  ## Home service now available

  - You can now get your motorcycle or scooter serviced at your home or workplace instead of visiting a service centre
  - The service itself is included in your rental — you only pay a travel fee based on your address
  - Available service locations include Algester, Coopers Plains, Yeronga, and Home Service
  - [Learn how home service works](/en/home-service)

  ## Direct debit verification via PayID (Email)

  - You can now verify your bank account for Direct Debit using a one-time $0.99 PayID payment
  - We generate a custom PayID email address for you to use — check the app or your setup communication
  - Step-by-step instructions are available for CommBank, NAB, Westpac, ANZ, St.George, and Suncorp
  - [See how to set up Direct Debit](/en/direct-debit)
</Update>

<Update label="2026-04-15" description="New documentation" tags={["New"]}>
  ## Charges & fees page now available

  - A new page listing all charges and fees that may apply during or after your rental
  - Covers damage excess, service and maintenance fees, return fees, administrative fees, and accessory replacement costs
  - [View charges and fees](/en/charges-fees)
</Update>

<Update label="2026-03-20" description="Language support" tags={["New"]}>
  ## Added French language support

  - Added French language option for the users to switch
  - Added French translation for French speaking customers
</Update>

<Update label="2026-03-10" description="Default Language" tags={["New"]}>
  ## English as default language

  - Added English language option as default
</Update>
