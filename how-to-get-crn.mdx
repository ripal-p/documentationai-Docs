title: How to get CRN (TMR)
description: >-
  Find your Queensland TMR Customer Reference Number, check existing documents,
  complete form F3503, and prepare for your licensing or transport transaction.
---
Your Customer Reference Number, or CRN, is issued by Queensland Transport and Main Roads (TMR). You may already have one on a Queensland driver licence or another TMR-issued product.

You need a CRN for some TMR licensing and transport transactions, including the Queensland motorcycle licensing process. Motion Rental does not require you to obtain a CRN as a general rental requirement.

<Expandable title="TL;DR" default-open="false">
  Check your existing TMR documents first. If you cannot find a CRN, complete the [New Customer Application (Individual), form F3503](https://www.support.transport.qld.gov.au/qt/formsdat.nsf/forms/QF3503/$file/F3503_CFD.pdf), then take the completed form and acceptable original identity documents to a [TMR customer service centre](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/contacts/centres).

  Queensland Government states that you can apply for a CRN at a TMR Customer Service Centre at no cost.
</Expandable>

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  TMR issues CRNs and controls the application process. Motion Rental cannot issue a CRN, confirm which identity documents TMR will accept, or guarantee the outcome of your application. Use the official TMR guidance for your situation.
</Callout>

## Get a CRN from TMR

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your existing TMR documents" title-type="p">
    Look for a CRN on your Queensland driver licence or another product or document issued by TMR. The [TMR CRN page](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/crn) explains where the number appears on TMR products and documents.

    If you find a CRN, keep the details available for your TMR licensing or transport transaction. You do not need to apply for another CRN.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete form F3503" title-type="p">
    If you cannot find an existing CRN, download and complete the [New Customer Application (Individual), form F3503](https://www.support.transport.qld.gov.au/qt/formsdat.nsf/forms/QF3503/$file/F3503_CFD.pdf).

    Complete the form with your current details. Do not assume that a document used for one TMR service will meet the requirements for another service.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take your application and identity documents to TMR" title-type="p">
    Take the completed application and acceptable original identity documents to a [TMR customer service centre](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/contacts/centres).

    Read TMR's [evidence-of-identity requirements](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/proof-of-identity-transport-services/individuals) before you attend. The documents TMR accepts can depend on the identity documents you hold.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Keep your CRN secure" title-type="p">
    Once TMR issues your CRN, store it securely with your other important transport details. You may need it for future TMR licensing or transport transactions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently asked questions

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What does CRN mean?" default-open="false">
    CRN means Customer Reference Number. TMR uses the number to identify you in its licensing and transport systems.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Why do I need a CRN?" default-open="false">
    You may need a CRN for TMR licensing and transport transactions, such as applying for a Queensland motorcycle licence. The CRN is the first step in the process described in the [QLD motorcycle licence](/en/qld-motorcycle-license) documentation.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Where can I find a CRN I already have?" default-open="false">
    Check your Queensland driver licence and other products or documents issued by TMR. The [official TMR CRN guidance](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/crn) shows where to look.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if I do not have a CRN?" default-open="false">
    Complete the [New Customer Application (Individual), form F3503](https://www.support.transport.qld.gov.au/qt/formsdat.nsf/forms/QF3503/$file/F3503_CFD.pdf) and take it to a TMR customer service centre with acceptable original identity documents.

    Review the [TMR evidence-of-identity requirements](https://www.qld.gov.au/transport/licensing/proof-of-identity-transport-services/individuals) before you attend.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Is there a fee to get a CRN?" default-open="false">
    Queensland Government states that you can apply for a CRN at a TMR Customer Service Centre at no cost. Other TMR services or transactions may have separate requirements or fees.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I get a CRN online?" default-open="false">
    The linked F3503 guidance is for a new customer application. An online-only application route was not verified for every situation, so contact TMR or check the current official guidance to confirm the options available to you.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
