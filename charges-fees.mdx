## TL;DR

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="TL;DR" default-open="true">
    This page lists the charges and fees that may apply during or after your rental with Motion Rental. Most fees only apply if something goes wrong, like missed services, lost accessories, or damage to the vehicle. We will always let you know before charging any fee, and it will be charged to the card or bank account you have on file with us.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## How charges work

Not all fees will apply to every rental. Charges only apply in specific situations, such as:

- Damage to the vehicle or accessories
- Missed or late services
- Lost items, including keys, accessories, or the number plate
- Returning the vehicle dirty or without fuel
- Tolls, fines, or declined payments

We will advise you before charging any fee. Full payment is expected within 7 days unless we agree to a payment plan with you in writing.

## Good to know

- We do not charge for fair wear and tear. Normal signs of use are expected and will never result in a fee.
- If you damage another vehicle or someone's property while riding, that is your responsibility — not something covered by your rental with us.
- Some charges can come through after you have returned the vehicle. Traffic fines, tolls, and damage found during the return inspection are the most common examples.
- You will always hear from us before any fee is charged. We will explain what happened, what the charge is for, and how much it is.
- If a fee applies and the full amount cannot be processed straight away, get in touch with us as soon as possible. We may be able to arrange a payment plan, but this needs to be agreed in writing and is not automatic.

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="How will I be charged if a fee applies?" default-open="true">
    We will advise you of the fee and charge it to the card or bank account you have on file with us. We prefer full payment to be processed immediately.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Is fair wear and tear charged?" default-open="false">
    No. Fair wear and tear is never charged. Fees for damage apply only when the damage goes beyond what would be expected from normal use.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if I can't pay the full amount straight away?" default-open="false">
    If we are unable to process the full amount, full payment is expected within 7 days. On a case-by-case basis, we may agree to a short payment plan if you contact us promptly. Payment plans are not automatic and must be agreed in writing.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Will I be told before a fee is charged?" default-open="false">
    Yes. We will always advise you of any applicable fee before charging it. For fees that apply after return, such as fines or damage found later, we will contact you to let you know.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Where can I see the full terms and conditions?" default-open="false">
    Full details are in your rental agreement. If you are unsure about any charge, contact us via WhatsApp before starting your rental so we can explain your options clearly.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
