## What is home service?

Home service gives you another way to complete your regular vehicle maintenance. Instead of bringing your scooter or motorcycle to one of our service centres, a mechanic comes to your home or workplace.

The actual service is included in your rental, so you do not pay extra for the servicing itself. You only pay a travel fee for the mechanic to attend your location.

## Service location options

When you book a service, you can choose from the available service locations on the form. Depending on the date and time selected, your options may include:

- Algester
- Coopers Plains
- Yeronga
- Home

Available locations can change based on the date and time you choose on the service form.

## How to check the travel fee

Check the travel fee on the [Book Service](https://motionrental.com.au/forms/service/#form) form.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Home Service as the location" title-type="p">
    In the **Service location** field on the service booking form, choose the Home Service option.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your address in the address field" title-type="p">
    As soon as you select Home Service, an address field appears on the form. Type your address into this field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the travel fee shown on the form" title-type="p">
    Once you enter your address, the travel fee is displayed directly on the service booking form.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requirements for home service

If you want your vehicle serviced at home or work, make sure the location is suitable before you book.

- The vehicle is in a garage or covered area so the mechanic can work protected from weather
- There is proper lighting so the mechanic can see clearly
- There is enough space for the mechanic to work around the vehicle comfortably
- There is a power point nearby for tools or equipment that need power
- The vehicle is clean and easy to access
- Any pets are secured away from the work area
- Someone is available at the address at the agreed time

## Make sure you select everything that needs servicing

When you book home service, include everything that needs servicing or replacing in the same booking. Check carefully before you submit the form.

<Callout kind="alert" collapsed="false">
  If you miss something and the mechanic needs to travel back a second time to fix or replace it, you will need to pay the travel fee again.
</Callout>

## What happens after you book

Once you book home service, the mechanic will call you to confirm the exact time and the servicing requirements. This call is used to finalise the details before the visit.

Be ready to confirm your address, access arrangements, and what needs to be done on the vehicle.

## Your responsibility

Booking service on time and getting the vehicle serviced when it is due remains your responsibility, even if you choose home service. Read [Service intervals](/en/service-intervals) if you need to check when your next service is due.

## FAQs

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Is the service cost included in my rental?" default-open="true">
    Yes. The actual servicing is included in your rental. You only pay a travel fee for the mechanic to come to your home or workplace.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How much is the travel fee?" default-open="false">
    The travel fee depends on your address. To check the exact price, select Home Service on the service form, enter your address, and the fee will be displayed.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Can I choose any location for service?" default-open="false">
    You can choose from Algester, Coopers Plains, Yeronga, or Home Service. Available locations may vary depending on the date and time you select.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if I forget to select something that needs servicing?" default-open="false">
    If you miss an item and the mechanic has to come back a second time, you will be charged the travel fee again. Double-check everything before submitting your booking.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Do I need to be home for the service?" default-open="false">
    Yes. Someone needs to be at the address at the agreed time to provide access and answer any questions the mechanic may have.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What if my home doesn't meet the requirements?" default-open="false">
    If your home does not have a garage, proper lighting, or enough space, book your service at one of our service centres instead. Available centre options may include Algester, Coopers Plains, or Yeronga.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
